# Brand Theme — Perpustakaan Hati

## Position
- **Business:** Perpustakaan Hati (The Library of the Heart)
- **Ambassador:** Rahimam, 10,500+ Threads followers
- **Promise:** Tulisan yang memulihkan (writing that heals)
- **Theme:** *Sanctuary in Gold* — dark sanctuary, antique gold, healing green
- **Series:** *Koleksi Ebook PERPUSTAKAAN HATI* — Siri Karya Jiwa & Kehidupan

## Visual narrative
The reference artwork is dark, warm, archival: ink-black grounds, antique gold
wordmarks, sepia panels, burgundy hearts. It reads like a private library at night.
That is the brand: **a lit room, a closed door, one warm lamp** — not a clinical
wellness site, not a bright self-help store.

Green enters here as the brand's healing signal. Olive is the only green that sits
beside antique gold without going clinical or "forest park". Olive marks *the moment
of calm* — the healing tier, quiet dividers, the pause in a conversation.

## Voice
- Empathetic, poetic but practical — Rahimam voice, BM-first with casual Malaysian English.
- Real-life relationship scenarios via *Rumah Tangga: Realiti & Rasa*, *Cerita Biasa Rasa Luar Biasa*, *Jalan Pulang*, *Ruang Tenang*, *Akar Hati*.
- Never judgmental, never clinical. Warmth first, framework second.
- Signature lines (from the artwork, keep verbatim):
  - "Karya untuk jiwa yang pernah terluka"
  - "Tulisan yang memulihkan"
  - "Sekali baca, lama terasa"
  - "Dari luka menjadi kata"
  - "Satu Koleksi. Pelbagai Rasa. Satu Jiwa."
  - "Merakam rasa, membina makna"
  - "Setiap perjalanan ada hikmah"
  - "Untuk jiwa yang ingin pulih"
  - "Teman hati sementara menunggu bas atau train" (Edisi Poket)
- Kicker/label voice: `— by @rahimam —`, `RAHIMAM SIGNATURE`, `Koleksi Karya, Kisah & Inspirasi`.

## Tier ladder (healing journey)
1. **Edisi Poket** (Free) — pocket diagnosis + fast relief, 15–25 pages.
2. **Edisi Membangun** (Standard) — actionable core guide, 60–90 pages.
3. **Teman Hidup** (Premium flagship) — bundle (Standard + 30-day workbook + scripts + audio), RM 79–RM 149.

## Emotional mapping
- Ink black — the sanctuary at night, safety, privacy, "read this alone"
- Antique gold — value, warmth, the curated shelf, premium
- Sepia — lived experience, memory, Malaysian home
- Burgundy — heartbreak, passion, the vulnerable tier
- Terracotta — action, urgency, conversion
- Cream — daylight, the calendar, the printable page
- Olive — healing, growth, the calm green accent

## Pillars of the ecosystem
1. *Sanctuary* — private, stigma-free, no judgment.
2. *Realiti Malaysia* — local scenarios, local payment rails, local helplines.
3. *Tulisan yang memulihkan* — every asset must leave the reader lighter.
4. *Independen* — no sponsor, no drama, no clickbait; only the work.
