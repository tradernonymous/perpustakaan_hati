# Tier Naming & Subcopy Lock

## Official tier ladder

| Store tier | Marketing name | Price (MYR) | Size / role | Tier colour |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free lead magnet | **Edisi Poket** | Free | 15–25 pages; pocket diagnostic + fast emotional reset; built-in bridge to paid tiers. | Green |
| Standard purchase | **Edisi Membangun** | RM 29–49 | 60–90 pages; actionable core guide + Malaysian scenarios. | Gold |
| Premium bundle | **Teman Hidup** | RM 79–149 | Standard + 30-day *Buku Kerja* + *Skrip Bincang Hati ke Hati* + Rahimam audio voice notes. | Burgundy |

## Subcopy (locked)
- **Edisi Poket** — "Ringkas, menyentuh, mudah dibawa." / "Teman hati sementara menunggu bas atau train."
- **Edisi Membangun** — "Panduan yang paling jujur dan paling mudah diikuti."
- **Teman Hidup** — "Bacaan, kerja, dan skrip — teman hidup." / "Untuk jiwa yang ingin pulih."

## Naming logic
- Each tier name is a **verb-stage in the healing journey**, not a product format: Poket (carry) → Membangun (build) → Hidup (live with).
- **Teman Hidup** replaces the retired *Edisi Pulih*. "Pulih" framed the buyer as broken and temporary; "Teman Hidup" frames the product as a lifelong companion and matches the flagship-bundle role.
- **Edisi Poket** replaces *Edisi Dompet* (seen in the reference artwork as "Handbag Edition"). The pocket metaphor is kept because the artwork already sells it; the public label drops "dompet" to avoid colliding with the payment/DuitNow word "dompet" in Malaysian checkout copy.

## Rules
- Do not run "Edisi Dompet" as a public tier. Keep it as an internal shorthand for commute-size pocket PDF exports only.
- Tier colour is fixed (green / gold / burgundy) and used consistently on cover, store card, and Threads card so the ladder is recognisable at a glance.
- Never rename a tier without updating: this file, `docs/brand/visual-identity.md`, the store product records, and any eBook cover template.
