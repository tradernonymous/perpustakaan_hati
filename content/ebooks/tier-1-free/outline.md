# Tier 1 — Edisi Poket (Free)

> "Teman hati sementara menunggu bas atau train."
> 15–25 pages · olive tier · RM 0

## Purpose
Pocket diagnostic + fast emotional reset. Built to be read in one sitting on a
commute, and to end with a natural reason to buy Edisi Membangun or Teman Hidup.

## Outline
1. **Halaman pertama** — Wordmark, "Untuk jiwa yang pernah terluka", one-line promise.
2. **Kenapa buku ini?** (1 p) — the sanctuary premise, in Rahimam's voice.
3. **Kad Refleksi** (4 p) — one prompt per page: Red Flag, Green Flag, Luka Hati, Corak Attach.
4. **Ceklis Pencetus Attach** (2 p) — 10-item self-check with scoring, points to which pattern to read next.
5. **7 Hari Reset** (7 p) — one micro-action per page, 60 seconds each, printable.
6. **Skrip Bicara** (1 p) — 3 short scripts for the hardest conversation this week.
7. **Pintu ke Edisi Membangun** (1 p) — self-evaluation checklist + QR to store.
8. **Sudut Keselamatan** (1 p) — Talian HEAL 15555, Befrienders, PDPA note.

## Bridge mechanic
Ends with a 5-item checklist whose answers map to one of three upgrade paths
(Edisi Membangun for the practical, Teman Hidup for the deep). QR + short code
printed on the back cover.
