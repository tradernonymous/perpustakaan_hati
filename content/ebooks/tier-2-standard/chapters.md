# Tier 2 — Edisi Membangun (Standard)

> "Panduan yang paling jujur dan paling mudah diikuti."
> 60–90 pages · gold tier · RM 29–49

## Purpose
The core actionable guide. Where Edisi Poket names the wound, this one trains
the reader to work with it. High-volume paid product.

## Structure
1. **Halaman judul** — wordmark, tier badge, gold.
2. **Kata pengarang** (2 p) — why this book exists, in the first person.
3. **Bab 1 — Kenapa hubungan sakit** — emotional regulation first; why we react before we think.
4. **Bab 2 — Kenali corak attach kamu** — anxious / avoidant / secure, in Malaysian scenarios (*Rumah Tangga: Realiti & Rasa*, *Cerita Biasa Rasa Luar Biasa*).
5. **Bab 3 — Red flag & green flag** — the shared vocabulary; a printed green-flag checklist.
6. **Bab 4 — Bila pertengkaran jadi pertapaan** — Gottman's four horsemen, reframed for BM casual speech.
7. **Bab 5 — Bicornak dengan jujur** — scripts for the four hardest conversations.
8. **Bab 6 — Bila dia tidak berubah** — boundaries, self-preservation, leaving well.
9. **Bab 7 — Pulih dengan perlahan** — heartbreak and grief, 30-day arc.
10. **Kit Refleksi** (12 p) — 30-question workbook, one prompt per page.
11. **Skrip Bincang Hati ke Hati** (preview of Teman Hidup) — 5 sample scripts.
12. **Pintu ke Teman Hidup** (1 p) — what the bundle adds; QR + short code.
13. **Sudut Keselamatan + PDPA**.

## Voice
BM-first, casual Malaysian English. Every framework introduced through a named
Malaysian scenario, never a generic "couple A and couple B".
