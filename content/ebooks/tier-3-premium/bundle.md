## Positioning
Not "a bigger book". The flagship promise is *continuity*: reading + doing +
having the words, plus a voice you can return to. "Teman Hidup" — the tier name
signals a long relationship with the reader, not a one-off purchase.

## Watermarking
Every page of every component carries `Cop Perpustakaan Hati` + buyer name/email.
Phase 2 watermarking engine produces a unique build per purchase.

## Upsell role
Appears as the 1-click order bump from Edisi Membangun at checkout and on the
thank-you page (Phase 3).
